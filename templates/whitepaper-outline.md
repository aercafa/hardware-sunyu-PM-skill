# Product Whitepaper Outlines

## Part A: Internal R&D Whitepaper

### 1. Document Control

- product family and version;
- file state and validation;
- owners and approval;
- source baselines;
- change history.

### 2. Product Definition

- problem and opportunity;
- users, customers, operators, and maintainers;
- value and non-goals;
- primary scenarios, journey, states, and timing;
- product-system framework.

### 3. Product Family

- family strategy;
- representative product;
- standard models;
- fixed, selectable, configurable, and custom attributes;
- naming and configuration rules.

### 4. Engineering Modules

- module purpose and scope;
- mechanical envelope;
- electrical, communication, firmware, and software interfaces;
- thermal, maintenance, diagnostics, and test;
- compatibility matrix;
- module version and change rules.

### 5. Standard Products

- scenario and positioning;
- configuration and selected modules;
- industrial design and interaction;
- structure, materials, installation, and maintenance;
- software, content, and service dependencies.

### 6. Requirements and Architecture

- product and engineering requirements;
- performance and reliability;
- safety, accessibility, privacy, and compliance;
- system and interface architecture;
- cost and supply boundaries.

### 7. Verification

- research evidence;
- proof-of-concept;
- prototype results;
- pilot-production results;
- requirement traceability;
- known limits and accepted risks.

### 8. Manufacture and Service

- BOM and approved alternatives;
- process and quality controls;
- firmware and configuration control;
- installation, update, rollback, maintenance, and spare parts.

### 9. Product Knowledge

- intellectual property;
- reusable assets;
- roadmap;
- owners and update process.

## Publication Rule

Every external claim must map to a current reviewed or tested internal baseline. Remove:

- confidential costs;
- supplier-sensitive information;
- unreleased architecture;
- security-sensitive details;
- unverified roadmap promises.
